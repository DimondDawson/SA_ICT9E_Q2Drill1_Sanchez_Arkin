A Guide to MCSR

DEFINE:

Short for "Minecraft Speedrunning", MCSR is commonly known as the practice of beating Minecraft (defeating the Ender Dragon) in the shortest time possible.

HISTORY:

MCSR has been around since 2010, but it gained popularity in 2019 when the first official speedrunning category was added to the game. Since then, it has become a popular competitive activity with a dedicated community of players.

RANKED:

A popular competitive mod and matchmaking platform (via MCSR Ranked) where players compete head-to-head on identical seeds with an Elo-based ranking system.

REGULATIONS:

  • Players must be knowledgable in Minecraft's gameplay to speedrun.
    This is common knowledge.
  • Players must use the Minecraft version that corresponds to their category.
  • Players must not use any mods or cheats that give them an unfair advantage.
    However, the Tool-Assisted Speedrun (TAS) category allows for the use of hacks or external programs, and often requires them.
  • Players must complete the game in survival mode.
  • Players must submit the raw unedited footage of their run.
  • The common requirement for a run to finish is for players to reach the End Poem sequence by going through the End Portal.
  • Players must defeat the Ender Dragon to complete the game.
    However, some run categories allow for certain glitches to be used in order to skip the Ender Dragon, often when the challenge deems fighting the Ender Dragon impossible.

OBJECTIVE SEQUENCE (standard run):

  1. Create a new world on, most advisably, v1.16.1 (VANILLA, gamemode "survival", allow commands "off", no seed input).
  2. The most advisable task as the timer starts is to gather the following in the sequence as swiftly as possible:
    wood (any type, +crafting table & wooden tools) -> stone (+furnace & stone tools) -> iron (+armor, tools, bucket)
  3. If you find one or more villages, obtain any useful contents of the chests, and obtain all the beds.
    If the village has a blacksmith building, collect the useful contents of the blacksmith chest--mostly iron ingots, armor and gear, and less commonly diamonds and obsidian.
  4. After you obtain a bucket, collect water from the nearest source. Afterwards, locate a lava pool and, using the water bucket and some stone-based blocks, create an improvised Nether portal.
    If you find a portal ruin, you can use it instead, or if you have obtained obsidian from a blacksmith village building, you can use them to fill in the rest of the portal structure.
  5. After creating the portal, light it with a flint-and-steel, or place a wooden plank of any type in the portal and let it catch fire. Then, enter it.
  6. After entering the Nether, equip at least ONE piece of gold armor to keep any Piglins neutral. Find a bastion remnant to barter with Piglins for Ender Pearls. Gold is required to trade with them and Ender Pearls have a low chance of being the item you get; however, there is a pile of gold blocks near the center of the structure.
    If your current version is beyond 1.16.1, be cautious in bastion remnants, specifically around Piglin Brutes--they attack you no matter what and deal significant damage.
  7. If you find a Crimson forest first (red), trade with the piglins there, or kill Endermen if you find a Warped forest (blue-green).
  8. After obtaining, advisably, 12-18 Ender Pearls, locate a fortress. After you find one, locate a Blaze spawner and kill the spawning Blazes. Their drops, Blaze Rods, are vital to the run.
    However, it is advised to craft a shield beforehand, as Blazes shoot fireballs.
  9. After obtaining the Ender Pearls and Blaze Rods, return to the Overworld and craft Ender Eyes by putting Blaze Rods in a crafting table to turn them into Blaze Powder, then combining the Ender Pearls and the Blaze Powder. It is advised you craft at least 14 Ender Eyes.
  10. After crafting the Ender Eyes, throw one. The Ender Eye will travel in the direction that the nearest stronghold is in. After some time, it will drop. Make sure to not lose any Ender Eyes. If the Ender Eye goes downwards, the stronghold is nearby or below you.
  11. After locating the stronghold, find the End Portal Room. There will be a square portal with 12 portal frames. Fill the empty portal frames with the Ender Eyes. After the portal initiates, enter it.
  12. After entering the End portal, you will spawn on an obsidian platform. You may spawn beside the island, or you may spawn within the island. No matter where you spawn, you must get on the main island, destroy the 10 explosive End Crystals that heal the Ender Dragon on the obsidian pillars (it is advised to use a bow and arrows), then wait for the Ender Dragon to perch at the center, place a bed obtained earlier on the perch and interact with them (beds cannot be slept on in the Nether and End, they explode, so make sure to protect yourself when doing this), and repeat it multiple times until you run out of beds or the dragon dies. If the dragon is still alive, keep hitting it with an iron or diamond sword until it dies. The dragon will ascend and die, which will open the final portal on the perch. Enter it to finish the run.
